## software architect & enterprise bridge-builder

Since 2011, I’ve worked as a Software Architect focusing on enterprise-scale integration, middleware, and API design/gateway infrastructure within the insurance sector. A core theme of my career is bridging the gap between legacy core systems and modern paradigms—connecting mainframe and COBOL environments with modern Java, Kubernetes, and JavaScript stacks.

## maker, machinist & outdoor cook

To balance out the digital world, I head to my homeshop to get my hands dirty. Beyond manual milling machines, lathes, 3D printers, and CNC retrofits, I love building custom hardware and software tools—you can check out some of my personal code projects on my GitHub.

When I’m not machining parts, coding personal builds, or wrenching on motorcycles, you’ll find me in my covered, weather-proof outdoor kitchen. I love cooking year-round—from low-and-slow BBQ to wood-fired pizza and everything in between.

## family & adventure

Off the clock, my ultimate priority is my family. I’m a proud father of three energetic boys—led by a pair of older twins. My boys live for two wheels just as much as I do; whether they're building backyard ramps, trying out bike tricks, or ripping around on their e-pit bikes to get a taste of motocross, life with them is an absolute blast.
